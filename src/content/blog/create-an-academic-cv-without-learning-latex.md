Academic CV builder: [https://cv.tanyaxing.com/](https://cv.tanyaxing.com/)

It is designed for students and researchers who are not familiar with LaTeX but need to create an English Academic CV.

Current features include:

- Freely adding and reordering sections
- Entering education, research, publications, projects, awards, and other experience
- Real-time CV preview
- Generating and downloading PDFs directly in the browser
- Importing and exporting JSON backups
- Automatically saving data in the current browser

No registration is required, and the information you enter is not uploaded to a server.
